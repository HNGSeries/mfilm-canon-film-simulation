Put M-Film on your camera

Step by step, no experience needed. About 15 minutes. Nothing in the camera changes for good: take the card out and it is Canon's camera again.

What you need

  • 📷Your camera, with a fully charged battery
  • 💾An SD card (a CF card on the 5D Mark II and 50D), 32 GB or smaller is best
  • 💻A computer with a card reader (Windows or Mac)
  • ⏱About 15 minutes, not in the middle of a shoot

  1. Check the camera's Canon firmware

    M-Film is made for one exact version of Canon's software. Turn the camera on and look:

    MENU→the last 🔧 settings tab→Firmware ver.
    It must say 2.0.2

    Different? Update the camera to exactly that version first, from Canon's support site for your camera. A newer or older one won't work.

  2. Set the camera to shoot RAW

    The films are made from the RAW file, so set this now, in Canon's menu, while it is still the normal camera:

    MENU→Image quality→RAW
    MENU→Image review→Off

    RAW+JPEG works too. A photo shot as JPEG only can't be developed.

  3. Download the file for your camera

    One zip file, about 18 MB. Pay what you want for it, $0 included.

    Download M-Film-camera-EOSM.202.zip

  4. Format the card in the camera

    Copy any photos you want to keep off the card first: formatting erases everything. Then, in the camera:

    MENU→Format card→OK

    Format it in the camera, not on the computer.

  5. Copy the files onto the card

    Put the card in the computer. Unzip the file you downloaded (Windows: right-click → Extract All; Mac: double-click it). That makes a folder: open it, select everything inside, and copy it onto the card.

    ✓ Right: on the card itself 📁 ML
    📄 autoexec.bin
    📄 ML-SETUP.FIR
    📄 M-FILM-README.txt
    ✗ Wrong: inside a folder 📁 M-Film-camera-…
       📁 ML
       📄 autoexec.bin
       📄 ML-SETUP.FIR

    Then eject the card (Windows: the USB icon → Eject; Mac: drag it to the bin) before you take it out.

    On a Mac: Mac's hidden "._" files can stop the camera. In Terminal, run dot_clean -m /Volumes/EOS_DIGITAL (your card's name), then eject.

  6. Run the installer, once

    Camera off, card in, camera on. Then:

    MENU→Firmware ver.→SET→OK

    Magic Lantern's installer appears and says when it's done (a few seconds). Switch the camera off.

    This camera has no installer on the card: it is done from a computer, with the free program mlinstall, over USB. It takes two clicks: Enable Boot Disk with the camera connected, then Write card boot flags with the card in the reader.

    The steps, with mlinstall →

    This doesn't replace Canon's firmware: it only tells the camera to start Magic Lantern from the card. You only do it once.

  7. Switch on: the film shelf appears

    Turn the camera on. After a moment you'll see M-Film's shelf of films. Pick one and press SET: the roll is loaded. Now shoot.

    This camera has no trash button: a short press of Av opens M-Film's home.

    The delete (trash) button opens M-Film's home; PLAY shows the developed prints. Canon's own menu is in M-Film: Home → Settings (INFO on Home goes straight there) → Canon menu.

    Practise on the camera in your browser first →

If something goes wrong

"Memory card containing firmware is required to update"

The camera didn't find the installer it accepts. Either the camera's firmware isn't exactly the version above (step 1), or the files are inside a folder instead of on the card itself (step 5). On a Mac, also run dot_clean (step 5).

"ML directory not found" / "Please copy all ML files"

The ML folder isn't next to autoexec.bin at the top of the card. Copy what is inside the unzipped folder, the ML folder included. Format in the camera first; with a card bigger than 32 GB, try a 32 GB one.

The camera won't start, or the screen stays black

Take the battery out, then the card. Put the battery back: the camera starts normally, as Canon's camera. Nothing is broken. Tell us which camera and firmware, so we can fix it.

Magic Lantern's own menu shows instead of the film shelf

On some cameras M-Film loads as a module after the camera starts, and it didn't load. The Modules tab says why on its line: tell us what it says.

How do I take it off?

Format the card in the camera. To stop the camera looking for Magic Lantern at all, run Firmware ver. → update again with the card in, and choose to remove the boot flag.
