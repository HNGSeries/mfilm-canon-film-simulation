# One photo, 68 films

One RAW from a Canon EOS M ([`ml-dev/films/sample/SAMPLE.CR2`](ml-dev/films/sample/SAMPLE.CR2), a street in late
afternoon sun), developed with every film on the shelf by the camera's own darkroom code at its Large size, then
made smaller for the page. Under each picture is the film stock's state.

<!-- film-states:start -->
**None of the 68 film stocks is measured against the real film yet.** 65 are in the style of their film and not measured yet. 2 (Kodak HIE Infrared, Rollei Infrared 400) are impressions of infrared film: a Canon with its infrared filter in place does not record what that film does. 1 (CineStill 400T) is M-Film's own and stands for no real film. A film stock counts as measured when its colour and tone come within the pass marks of a lab scan of a roll of the real film, shot beside a Canon: [how a film stock becomes measured](docs/paired-shot.md).
<!-- film-states:end -->

Where the looks come from until a film stock is measured: for 59 of them, a film emulation table by
[Pat David](https://patdavid.net) ([CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), through G'MIC and
[Film-Luts](https://github.com/YahiaAngelo/Film-Luts)), a look made by eye; for the rest, M-Film's own model. Grain,
halation and softness are set per film stock, by eye as well.

[`ml-dev/films/pictures.py`](ml-dev/films/pictures.py) makes every picture here, so they can be made again when
the colour changes. [All of them on one sheet](docs/films/grid.jpg).
