# mfilm.app: home, install guide, 404

Scope: site/index.html (Persuade), site/install.html (Read, a step-by-step form), site/404.html. Visitor: a Canon
owner who plays with the camera in the browser, then downloads M-Film and puts it on the card (in Chrome, Edge
or Opera from the install page itself). Keep every function, string (English and Thai) and the film OS screens.
Quality bar named by the maintainer: Leica and Fujifilm X / Instax product pages. Motion: film-camera gestures
only at key moments.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A camera maker's product page, executed with Leica and Fujifilm restraint: the working camera is the
product shot and the 68 developed frames are the photography. It refuses the gadget-landing kit: gradient
glow, badge rows, emoji icons, pill tabs, a wall of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (#0b0b0a) with one lifted surface; warm neutral ink; every grey from one ramp;
amber #eca834 the only colour, kept for actions, the active state and the progress line; camera status told
by the dot's form (filled, half, open ring) and its word; one error red, for errors only. Barlow at 500 and
600 for everything, large and quiet, tabular figures in data; Noto Sans Thai beside it. Hairline rules, square
photographs, 6px corners on controls and none on images.

STORY: The visitor sees the real camera, presses its buttons, loads a roll and shoots; scrolls into one big
photograph that re-develops as they pick films; checks their camera in a spec table; downloads; installs from
the form, one step at a time.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (logo, links, TH, Download). Centred headline "Your Canon, shot like film." on one
line at 76px (two on narrow screens) with a short lede and two actions: Download for your Canon (amber) and
How to install. Under it the playable camera at 760px, whole within 1440x900; under the camera, on one
hairline, its status and dial keys, then the camera picker and the keyboard keys; the four try-it steps run in
one row beneath and light up as they are done.

FORM: The category standard, played straight (canon chosen on the decision page); seed: seed:90455249631d8282.
Signature interaction: the film viewer, one large frame that re-develops (latent to developed) when a film is
picked. Motion grammar: a focal-plane shutter curtain between install steps, a frame counter for the step
number, the developing reveal for photographs; everything else 150-250ms fades.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict,
DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
